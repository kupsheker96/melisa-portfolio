Hub Gym

Gym management app

The Hub Gym App is a scheduling and fitness management app, simplifying the planning and tracking of workouts, trainer sessions, and other service appointments.

As the sole designer, I was responsible for the user interface and experience design. I also guided and managed the team, ensuring a strong design focus and fostering collaboration to achieve our project goals.

05.2 — Process

The Human-Centered Design (HCD) Process

[ Discovery & Research ]
Problem Definition

Identified the primary challenge: to create an intuitive platform that simplifies planning and progress tracking for gym clients and trainers, enhancing motivation and engagement.

User Research & Analysis

Conducted extensive user research through surveys and observation to gather insights on user needs and pain points. Analyzed the data to identify patterns and key user behaviors.

[ Ideation & Planning ]
Brainstorming

Held brainstorming sessions with the team to generate a wide range of ideas and solutions. Categorized and prioritized ideas based on feasibility and user impact.

User Flow

Created a comprehensive user flow diagram to visualize the step-by-step interactions within the app.

[ Design & Development ]
Wireframing

Developed low-fidelity wireframes to outline the app’s structure and layout.

UI Design

Designed a visually appealing and user-friendly interface that reflects the modern and energetic brand of Hub Gym.

05.3Research

Qualitative Research

To gain insights into the user experience we conducted a comprehensive online survey with the current members. Analyzing the responses revealed key pain points and preferences, informing our design decisions for the Hub Gym App.

“I don't even know which trainer works on which day, let alone their hours.”
“When I take a break, it is hard to remember how many classes I have left.”
“The operation is not transparent.”
