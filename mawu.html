Mawu

Storytelling Tool for Play Therapy

Mawu is a haptic storytelling and emotional regulation tool for play therapy sessions.

It includes different activity pebbles and characters for children to create their stories and help them understand emotions better with sensory balls.

06.1Context

What is Play Therapy?

It is a type of therapy where play and art materials are used as the main way for people to express themselves. Play therapy can be nondirective, allowing a client to play, or the play can be directed by the therapist.

“Play therapy has a preventative effect on anxiety and worry levels. It has individualized effects on each child, which makes sense since the way a child plays is unique to them.”(Stulmaker & Ray, 2015)

06.2 — Research

Research

[ Qualitative Research ] · 05 chapters

Throughout Mawu's development, I conducted user research with parents and play therapists.

Insights revealed the need for sensory play, child-centered approaches, and managing emotional regulation.

Surveys showed preferences for physical play and role-playing, highlighting toy durability and engagement challenges, which informed and refined Mawu's design.

[ Brief ]How can we make a tool that helps children and therapists work together to understand and regulate emotions in play therapy sessions?

06.6Prototype

Mockup Design

Utilizing 3D printing, laser-cut MDF, wood, and custom stickers, Mawu features activity pebbles and characters that enable children to craft their stories and explore emotions through sensory balls.

Each emotion ball is crafted from materials chosen for their unique texture, temperature, and haptic impact, offering a diverse sensory experience.

This diverse material selection enhances emotional understanding and regulation in a playful and engaging manner.
