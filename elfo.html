02

Elfo

AI-Powered Storytelling App for Children

Elfo is an app that lets children create personalized stories with AI. Children say what kind of story they want, and the app generates a story with accompanying sounds based on their wishes.

The concept and design were developed from scratch in a 48-hour collaborative sprint by a team of four.

Type
Collaborative sprint
Time
48 Hours
Team
4 People
Role
UI/UX & Visual Designer
Result
Working demo
01Problem

The product asks for a lot of input to personalize a story: the child’s age, interests and goals, and for each story its length, theme, lesson, narrator and illustration style. The design challenge was to turn these choices into a short, guided flow.

02What I did

UI/UX and visual design · Elfo name & logo · AI-generated illustrations

03Outcome

By the end of the sprint, the developers had built a working demo of the app.

There was no time for user testing within the 48 hours, and the project did not continue after the sprint.

02.4UI/UX design

Interface details

HEX #29185F HEX #112363 HEX #7232DE HEX #F1E77C
  1. 01Step progressA segmented bar under “Create a Story”, and 1/4–3/4 counters in profile setup, show where the user is in the flow.
  2. 02Two clear optionsStory length is a choice between “3-5 min (short)” and “5-10 min (long)”.
  3. 03Optional free input“What is the story about?” is marked optional, so a story can be made without typing.
  4. 04One primary actionEvery step ends with a single full-width “Continue” button.
05Choosing by pictureJourneys, narrators and illustration styles are shown as illustrated tiles. A “Shuffle” or “Randomize” tile lets the app choose.
06Showing the AI at workWhile the story is generated, a checklist names each stage and the wait is stated up front.
  • ✓ Understanding your child’s interests
  • ✓ Crafting the perfect characters
  • ✓ Painting the illustrations...
  • ✓ Adding the magic finale
  • “Usually takes about 10 seconds”
