08.3Usage Scenario

hubgenetik.com

01Activate Your Kit

Scan the QR code provided in the kit and follow the activation instructions.

02Create Your Account

After creating your account, you are ready to collect your sample. Do not proceed without creating an account. Your test results will be accessible through your account.

03Collect Your Sample

With clean hands, remove the cotton swab from its packaging. Gently swab the inside of your cheek for 30-60 seconds, then repeat on the other cheek.

04Secure the Sample

Insert the swab into the vial, apply gentle pressure to break off the excess part, and ensure the vial is securely closed. Repeat this process with the second swab.

05Prepare for Shipping

Place the vials into the pre-addressed shipping package included in the kit.

06Ship Your Sample

Drop off your package at the nearest Yurtiçi Kargo branch for free, or choose the courier option for pickup.
